## Relevant source facts, paraphrased

- Adaptive v2 probes rising flow after preinfusion for 7 seconds, learns the highest flow achieved while pressure remains below 9 bar, then continues at that flow.
- The guide's desired Adaptive v2 example calls for peak pressure of 8–9 bar and a shot near 25 seconds. Its example graph is a saved shot, not a control-target graph.
- Londonium (LRv2) starts with a 3 bar saturation phase, rises toward 9 bar, then gradually declines. If flow is too high near the end, the recipe transitions from pressure control to flow control to slow the shot.
- The linked successful LRv2 graph is a measured 33-second shot. Its visible table reports 14 seconds of preinfusion and 19 seconds of extraction. These are observations from that shot, not required recipe timings.
